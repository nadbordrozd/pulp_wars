## In the game

Play with the chibi art set at https://nadbordrozd.github.io/pulp_wars/?art=chibi (the choice is
remembered; `?art=legacy` switches back). Zoom with + and - or pinch; the
steps are 0.75, 1, 1.5 and 2.
