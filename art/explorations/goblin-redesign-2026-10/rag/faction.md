# Faction fragment: TEST-GOBLIN-RAG (exploration)

**Status:** TEST ONLY, not yet generated. Direction B, "Rust-red rags and
canvas", of the Goblin redesign study (bead `pulp_wars-wrn.1`, see
[GOBLIN_REDESIGN.md](../../../../docs/art/factions/GOBLIN_REDESIGN.md)): the
alternative to the recommended direction A, ready to run if the user
prefers a saturated cloth accent to the hazard-paint one. A fresh creation
of every Goblin piece; it is not a faction and never registers production
art.

The faction layer names only a mood, materials, colours and small motifs.
The hex values are the palette of
`scripts/art/goblin-redesign/directions.ts`.
