# Faction fragment: TEST-HUMAN-ACCENT (exploration copy)

**Status:** TEST ONLY. The faction layer of the visual-direction study (bead
`pulp_wars-3tq.1`, see
[VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md](../../../docs/art/VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md)).
It is not a faction and never produces production art.

The study asks whether Human units read better with a fixed faction
identity (cream cloth and light steel) and the player colour kept to one
small, well placed accent (a crest, a shield face, a hood, a plume, a
pennant) instead of whole garments. The recipes are edits of accepted
production sprites, so the silhouettes stay and only the colours move. The
accent is still painted in the key red `#d8262c`, so the runtime mask
recolour works unchanged; only the mask gets smaller.

The two text blocks below are the production ORIGINAL fragment, unchanged:
the study changes where the owner colour sits, not the faction's materials.

## Prompt fragment

```text
Faction: cheerful, practical storybook medieval. Everything is made of woven
cloth, light steel, pale birch wood, cream plaster and beige cobblestone,
with stitched hems, steel rivets and carved trims as details; cloth is
shaded with a deeper tone of its own colour, steel with darker blue-grey,
wood with darker straw yellow, plaster and stone with cool grey, never brown.
```
