# Faction fragment: TEST-DINOSAUR-PRIMAL (exploration copy)

**Status:** TEST ONLY. Dinosaur units in fixed faction colours, with no
owner-colour area: slate to steel blue hides with warm cream bellies, tanned
cavemen in tawny furs, and exactly one hot accent, amber-orange. It is the
faction layer of the Dinosaur visual-direction study (bead
`pulp_wars-3tq.14`, see
[VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md](../../../docs/art/VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md#18-dinosaur-study)).
It is not a faction and never produces production art. Unlike
[DINOSAUR.md](../../../docs/art/factions/DINOSAUR.md) it allows tan fur,
brown spots, tanned skin and orange: with no key colour to extract and no
player colour on the sprite, a drift towards brown, Gold or Coral no longer
breaks a mask or names a player.

Every recipe of the study is an `edit-image-pixen` edit of an accepted
Dinosaur sprite, and an edit sends only its instruction, so this fragment
is the written look that the instructions follow rather than text PixelLab
receives.

## Prompt fragment

```text
Faction: cheerful prehistoric lost-world storybook, playful and never scary.
Their fixed colours: smooth slate to steel blue dinosaur hide, darker navy
along the back, with warm cream bellies, jaws, teeth and claws; tanned
cavemen with dark hair in tawny spotted furs with bone and tooth jewellery;
and exactly one hot accent colour, a bright amber-orange, only on feather
crests, frills, body markings and war paint. Hide may carry one bold simple
pattern: a few thick stripes, a few big spots, or a dark saddle.
```
