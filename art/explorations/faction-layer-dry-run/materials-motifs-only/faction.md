# Faction fragment: TEST-CLOCKWORK (throwaway test faction)

**Status:** TEST ONLY. A throwaway faction for the faction-layer dry run
(bead `pulp_wars-tt3.1`), written from
[FACTION_TEMPLATE.md](../../../../docs/art/factions/FACTION_TEMPLATE.md). It
is not a real faction and must never produce production art.

This is the recommended arm: the prompt fragment names only era, materials,
surfaces, colours and small motifs. It names no figure and no building, so
no whole subject can leak from one class into another. The unit subject
lines carry the body language and the City subject line carries the
architecture. Compare the
[bodies-in-fragment](../bodies-in-fragment/faction.md) control and the
[materials-only](../materials-only/faction.md) arm, whose fragment adds one
architecture sentence.

## Identity

Wind-up tin-toy automatons, chosen to be as far as possible from the
storybook medieval humans: metal instead of cloth and wood, boxy robots
instead of people, domed workshops instead of cottages.

## Prompt fragment

```text
Faction: TEST clockwork, a wind-up tin-toy world. Everything is made of
riveted dark blue-grey gunmetal plates and ivory-white enamel panels, with a
few small brass rivets and gear teeth, small round glass portholes and little
wind-up keys as details.
```

## Negative fragment

```text
wood, timber, thatch, straw, plaster, cottage, stone keep, copper, rust,
orange, magic glow, gun, rifle, pistol
```

## Palette

- Owner colour: the shared key colour on enamel chest plates, hats, domes and
  banners.
- Secondary colours: dark blue-grey gunmetal, ivory-white enamel, and a
  little brass trim (brass stays small: it sits close to the Gold player
  colour).

## Silhouette language

- Boxy tin-can bodies, one big round porthole eye, a wind-up key on the back.
- Settlements are drum-shaped domed workshops round a clock tower.
