# Faction fragment: TEST-CLOCKWORK, bodies-in-fragment control

**Status:** TEST ONLY. The control arm of the faction-layer dry run (bead
`pulp_wars-tt3.1`). It is the same throwaway faction as
[materials-only](../materials-only/faction.md), with the prompt fragment
written the way the Human fragment is: bodies first, then towns. Only the
City is generated from it, with the same seed, subject and negatives, to see
whether figure words in layer 3 put figures into settlements.

## Identity

Wind-up tin-toy automatons (see the materials-only arm).

## Prompt fragment

```text
Faction: TEST clockwork wind-up tin soldiers; boxy chunky automaton bodies of
riveted dark blue-grey gunmetal with a round glass porthole eye instead of a
face, ivory-white enamel panels, small brass rivets and gear teeth and a big
wind-up key on the back; towns of drum-shaped riveted iron workshops with
domed roofs, a clock tower with a big white clock face, pipes and short
chimney stacks.
```
