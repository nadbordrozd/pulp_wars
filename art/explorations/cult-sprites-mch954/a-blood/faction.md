# Faction fragment: TEST-CULT-BLOOD (exploration)

**Status:** exploration run of bead `pulp_wars-mch9.54`, round 2 of the
robed cultists, variant A, "blood livery". The user (2026-10-17), on the
round-1 samples: "thematically, the cultists are schmucks who learned some
incantations. That's why I don't want them to have tentacles or skeletal
faces. etc. I guess some high priest might show signs of corruption but in
general they're just dudes with fancy robes while their summons are weird
and otherwordly. I want their evil be portrayed through evil looking robes
and bloody daggers and such."

Every robed cultist is a fresh creation: ordinary people with visible human
faces, the evil carried by the costume and the props. Variant A dresses the
society mostly in deep blood red (black and deep purple as the second
colour of some roles); variant B (`../b-black`) mostly in black. The run is
not a faction and registers nothing; what was imported into production is
listed in [CULT.md](../../../../docs/art/factions/CULT.md).

## Prompt fragment

```text
Faction: a pulp-magazine secret society of the 1920s, sinister, grim and

wax and parchment; bright red fresh blood on blades; and one small accent, a
glowing spring green, only as candle flames and sparks.
```
