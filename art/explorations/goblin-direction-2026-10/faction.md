# Faction fragment: TEST-GOBLIN-SCRAPYARD (exploration copy)

**Status:** TEST ONLY. Goblin units as scrapyard raiders in fixed faction
colours, with no owner-colour area. It is the faction layer of the Goblin
visual-direction study (bead `pulp_wars-3tq.8`, see
[VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md](../../../docs/art/VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md#14-goblin-study)).
It is not a faction and never produces production art. Unlike
[GOBLIN.md](../../../docs/art/factions/GOBLIN.md) it allows brown leather,
rust and wood: with no key colour to extract, red-brown drift no longer
breaks a mask.

## Prompt fragment

```text
Faction: rowdy, ramshackle scrapyard raiders, loud and silly, never grim.
Their fixed colours: near-black dark brown leather and patched hide with
dirty cream bandage wraps; mismatched rusted iron and dark gunmetal scrap
plates with bolts; one signature accent of hazard yellow and black diagonal
warning stripes, only as a small striped band on dark scrap, never a solid
yellow garment; tiny red-orange fuse sparks. Leather is shaded near-black,
iron with darker blue-grey, rust is a dull orange-brown.
```
