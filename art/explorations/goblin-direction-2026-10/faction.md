# Faction fragment: TEST-GOBLIN-SCRAPYARD (exploration copy)

**Status:** TEST ONLY. Goblin units as scrapyard raiders in fixed faction
colours, with no owner-colour area. It is the faction layer of the Goblin
visual-direction study (beads `pulp_wars-3tq.8` and `pulp_wars-3tq.10`, see
[VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md](../../../docs/art/VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md#14-goblin-study)).
It is not a faction and never produces production art. Unlike
[GOBLIN.md](../../../docs/art/factions/GOBLIN.md) it allows brown leather,
rust and wood: with no key colour to extract, red-brown drift no longer
breaks a mask.

The fragment below is the **pass 2** look (bead `pulp_wars-3tq.10`): the
user asked for darker skin, much less dark clothing, orange-brown leather
loincloths and a fireworks cart. Only the fresh creations of pass 2 use it;
edits send their own instruction. The pass 1 fragment is kept at the end as
history: `records.json` holds each request exactly as it was sent.

## Prompt fragment

```text
Faction: rowdy, ramshackle goblin raiders, primitive, loud and silly, never
grim. Their fixed colours: deep true goblin green skin with a lot of bare
skin showing; warm orange-brown leather loincloths, straps, harnesses,
wrist wraps and small caps; weathered brown planks and pale rope; a little
rusted iron and dark gunmetal scrap; home-made fireworks in grubby red,
orange, yellow, blue and green paper with cream paper cones; near-black
only in small details such as a belt or a round bomb; tiny red-orange fuse
sparks. Leather is shaded dark red-brown, skin with darker green.
```
