# Faction fragment: TEST-HUMAN-HERALDIC (exploration copy)

**Status:** TEST ONLY. Human units in fixed high-medieval heraldic colours (crimson and gold on steel), with no owner-colour area. It is the faction layer of the Human demo of
the visual-direction study (bead `pulp_wars-3tq.3`, see
[VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md](../../../../docs/art/VISUAL_DIRECTION_2026-10.md)).
