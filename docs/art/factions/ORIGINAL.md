# Faction fragment: ORIGINAL (Humans)

**Status:** baseline faction for the chibi migration. The tile-80 test
Fighter and Marksman, which the user chose, show the intended look.

## Identity

Grounded storybook medieval humans: settlers, soldiers and builders. They are
the game's baseline, organised and practical, with no magic (see the
[tech tree design principles](../../product/PULP_WARS_TECH_TREE_DESIGN_PRINCIPLES.md)).

## Prompt fragment

```text
Faction: cheerful storybook medieval humans; simple cloth tunics and hoods,
rounded steel helmets with crests, wooden shields and bows, leather boots and
belts; towns of timber-and-plaster cottages with tile roofs, round stone keeps
and short stone walls; no magic, no firearms, no modern or Napoleonic items.
```

## Negative fragment

```text
gun, musket, cannon on units, top hat, bicorne, tricorne, modern clothing,
steel plate full armour, magic glow, wings, pedestal, base, ground plate
```

## Palette

- Owner colour: the shared key colour on tunics, hoods, roofs, banners and
  sails.
- Secondary colours: steel grey, warm wood brown (clearly not red), and cream
  or plaster.

## Silhouette language

- Stocky big-headed humans with one oversized signature item per role.
- Towns have round keeps and red (owner-colour) roofs.
- Ships are small wooden sailing craft.

## Roster notes

| Role or building | Distinguishing silhouette                                                                     |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fighter          | crested helmet, oversized short sword, round shield                                           |
| Raider           | rider on a small pony, spear or axe, riding cloak                                             |
| Marksman         | pointed hood, tall longbow, quiver, no shield                                                 |
| Guard            | big tower shield and spear, heavy helmet, wide stance                                         |
| Captain          | feathered hat or officer cap (medieval), banner or horn, no shield                            |
| Catapult         | wooden catapult with a small crew figure                                                      |
| Knight           | armoured rider on a horse, lance, horse barding in owner colour                               |
| Juggernaut       | giant armoured brute with a huge shield or hammer                                             |
| Patrol Boat      | small single-mast sailing boat with an owner-colour sail                                      |
| Battleship       | larger two-mast warship, a medieval galleon or carrack                                        |
| City and village | village: two to three cottages; city: cottages round a stone keep, growing in size with level |

Building-specific notes (Windmill, Sawmill, Forge, Workshop, Market, Port,
Shipyard, Farm, Lumber Camp, Mine, Monument) are set in the batch beads and
the asset inventory. Each needs a unique silhouette at zoom 0.75.
